Fasari karanǝn hanzarṅ, amma shima be karanǝ. Simon-Galleria sǝdǝn hoto-a bidio-a app kura kǝntǝ, ngǝrza-a sirri-a. Adda tallabǝ, adda izni tuwo-a – kurǝ shima bǝlan tǝmata.

**🖼️ HOTO GIYA KƏLA LUBƏ NƏM:**  
Hoto cidǝn hauwǝ sǝdǝ editordǝ mai basi amma mai ngǝwu. Gǝrǝm, sabtǝ, njinǝm, kǝlaftǝ, gwadǝm, sakin filtersǝ sǝmba kǝla, sirri shima ad sammnǝ. Nǝm karanǝ shimǝ la kǝla nanka gǝnyi.

**🌐 SIRRI KƏRA FƏLƏ, SA'A SUWAN:**  
Sirri shimǝ ngǝwu. Kǝla mata-a ngǝwu-a dǝga shima. Simon-Galleria shima ganatǝ kǝra. Kǝskǝm EXIF meta-data yayen GPS-a kamera-a, karanǝ shimǝ la shima kǝla.

**🔒 GƏROLAN MILTƏ:**  
Karanǝ shimǝ la kǝntǝ PIN, hoto, wala chikin kǝra. Hoto, bidio, wala app kura kǝntǝ kǝla – shima faltǝ wande nguwo kǝla. Kǝndǝl kǝla, tabtǝ.

**🔄 BƏRTƏM CEWA:**  
Fǝlǝ, asǝla bǝlan ndǝro! Simon-Galleria recycle bin kǝntǝ shima riwo hoto-a bidio-a kauta saniya. Kǝla kǝrtǝ tuwu, amma fǝlǝ wo.

**🎨 GALLERY SHIMƏ, STYLE SHIMƏ:**  
Kǝla suitǝm, ngǝma-a awo-a, style shimǝ kǝla. UI themes-a function buttons-a, Simon-Galleria shima ṣai nguwo tilisǝ kǝla.

**📷 FORMAT-A SƏLLƏMƏ NGUWO:**  
JPEG, JPEG XL, PNG, MP4, MKV, RAW, SVG, GIF, AVIF, bidio-a-a, dǝwa – kǝla format nguwo ndǝro, karanǝ shimǝ kǝntǝ. Adda adǝmmana, kurǝ wande tuwu.

**✨ MATERIAL DESIGN THEMES-DƏ NGUWO:**  
Ngǝrza kǝla material design themes-a, kǝla suitǝm. Awo ngǝwu? Custom themes-a cewo, gallery shimǝ kǝla nanka kǝla.
