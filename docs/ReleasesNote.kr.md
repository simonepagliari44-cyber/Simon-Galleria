## 📌 **Bayani**

**Simon-Galleria** sǝdǝ gallery hoto-a bidio-a native **Android**, i nguwo yebgado kǝla ngǝma-a karanǝ-nza ndǝ tǝmata-a shima ad dǝgǝnyi. Adda kǝladǝ, adda tracking, adda izni tuwo-a: kurǝ shima bǝlan tǝmata nguwo.

Shima tǝmǝn hoto-a bidio-a shimǝ-a, tǝwoltǝ-a tǝsǝftǝ-a, tǝlǝbu agbur shimǝ mai sirri-a yerna tǝyuwo kǝla kǝrtǝ-a saniya sǝdǝ recycle bin-a. Kwala tǝmǝn **sǝ wu shimǝn suro**, sǝ sirri ngǝwu.

---

## ✨ **Cidǝwa**

* 🖼️ **Editor hoto-a nguwo cidǝn-a:** gǝrǝm, sabtǝ, njinǝm, kǝlaftǝ, gwadǝm-a filters-a cidǝn app-a.
* 🔐 **Sirri kǝra fǝlǝ:** kǝskǝm EXIF metadata yayen GPS-a kamera-a.
* 🔒 **Gǝrolan miltǝ:** kǝntǝ hoto-a, bidio-a wala app kura sǝdǝ PIN, hoto-a fingerprint-a.
* ♻️ **Recycle bin-a nguwo cidǝn-a:** riwo hoto-a bidio-a kauta sǝdǝ asǝla-a saniya.
* 🎨 **Themes dynamic-a:** Material Design sǝdǝ themes light/dark-a system-a, sǝdǝ custom themes-a.
* 📷 **Formats-a sǝllǝmǝ-a cidǝn-a:** JPEG, JPEG XL, PNG, MP4, MKV, RAW, SVG, GIF, AVIF-a dawo.
* 🔍 **Cǝkta-a folders-a:** cǝkta media saniya-a gǝmnǝ folders-a nguwo, kǝla-a, nga-a yefǝ-a.
* 🖥️ **Wallpaper-a widget-a:** sǝtǝn hoto wallpaper-a-a sakin folders widget home screen-a.
* 📴 **Adda connection-a so-a:** app-a tǝmǝn **offline**.

## 🔐 **Sirri-a gǝrol**

Sirri-i fatǝ app-a: adda account, adda data-a nguwo gǝmna, adda connection be wu-a. Cidǝwa lock-a tǝmnen app kura wala agbur nguwo shimǝ faltǝ, amma kǝskǝ-a EXIF metadata-a sara-a tǝmǝn shima location-a wala cidǝwa-a ndǝ-a asǝla.

| Izni | Sa'a | Ngoji |
|---|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` | Android 13+ | Kǝnǝ hoto-a bidio-a sǝ wu-a |
| `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED` | Android 14+ | Pīeja nguwo fail-a faltǝ-a |
| `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` | sa'a suwan | Kǝnǝ location-a sǝ EXIF metadata-a |
| `SET_WALLPAPER` | sǝ nǝm-a | Sǝtǝn hoto wallpaper-a |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Android 13+ | Nǝm-a app-a |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | ndǝ Android 10 | So-a tǝsǝftǝ media-a |
| `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | optional | Gǝmnǝ fail-a kura |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | sa'a suwan | Refresh ka lista media-a sǝ reboot-a |

App-a **adda** so-a izni network ne camera.
