## 📌 **Tamuɣli tamatut**

**Simon-Galleria** d ttemkar n tiwlafin d ividyuten **Android** aneṣli, yettwaxdem i medden i yebɣan anbaḍ amkul ɣef timkta-nsen war ma ǧǧin tawuri. War iɣewwaɣen, war aḍfar, war tisirag ur ilaqen: ɣas tirmit tuzzya, tettwaxdam i keč.

Tzemred ad twalid, ad teseggem, ad tesewjed tiwlafin d ividyuten-nnek, ad teffred agbur ameqqran, daɣen ad talseḍ ayen yettwakkes deg kra n tesdatin s ussuddes n usefrek. Kullci iteddu **srid ɣef ubadaw-nnek**, s tudert tusligt tummidt.

---

## ✨ **Timahilin**

* 🖼️ **Asewjed n tewlaft azerfan:** gzem, beddel tiddi, susem, qleb, ɣer daɣen sseqdec ifiltren deg asnas.
* 🔐 **Tudert tusligt d tamezwarut:** kkes metadata EXIF am tikwatin GPS d isallen n tkamirat.
* 🔒 **Taɣellist tẓayrit:** sɣel tiwlafin, ividyuten neɣ asnas akk s PIN, asekkil neɣ adhm n uḍad.
* ♻️ **Asefrek azerfan:** alsi tiwlafin d ividyuten i yettwakkesen s tɣellist deg kra n tesdatin.
* 🎨 **Themes tuddin:** Material Design s themes asfaw d uberkan i iṭṭafaren anagraw, daɣen themes usligen.
* 📷 **Aṭas n ifmaten yettwaɛeqqlen:** JPEG, JPEG XL, PNG, MP4, MKV, RAW, SVG, GIF, AVIF d wiyaḍ.
* 🔍 **Anadi d yiserdas:** anadi n umidya s zzerb d usefrek n yiserdas yettwarnan, yettwakkasen d yuffren.
* 🖥️ **Tisɣart d uwijit:** sers tawlaft am tisɣart-nnek daɣen rnu awijit n userdas ɣef ugdud n uxxam.
* 📴 **War tazmert n tuqqna:** asnas iteddu **s war tuqqna** tummidt.

## 🔐 **Tudert tusligt d tɣellist**

Tudert tusligt d wul n usnas: war amiḍan, war isefka yettwaleqḍen, war tuqqna ɣer beṛṛa. Timahilin n usɣel ḥerzent asnas akk neɣ ɣas agbur i tfernded, ma d tukksa n metadata EXIF tḥettem war beṭṭu n wemkan-nnek neɣ isallen n tkarḍit s tɣellist.

| Tasiragt | Melmi | I wacu |
|---|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` | Android 13+ | Ɣer tiwlafin d ividyuten ɣef ubadaw |
| `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED` | Android 14+ | Anekcum yefren ɣer yifuyla yettwafren |
| `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` | dima | Ɣer amkan seg metadata EXIF |
| `SET_WALLPAPER` | ɣef usuter | Sers tawlaft am tisɣart |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Android 13+ | Tilɣa n usnas |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | ɣer Android 10 | Sekles daɣen sewjed amidya |
| `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | d afrayan | Asefrek amkul n yifuyla |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | dima | Aleqqem n umuɣ n umidya deffir usiɣzef |

Asnas **war** yeḥwaǧ ara tasiragt n uẓeṭṭa neɣ anekcum ɣer tkamirat.
