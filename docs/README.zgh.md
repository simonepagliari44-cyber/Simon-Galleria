Fk awed timkta, maci isefka udmawanen. Simon-Galleria d asnas aneggaru n tewlaft d uvidyu i izeddig u i uslig. War iɣewwaɣen, war tisirag ur ilaqen – ɣas tirmit tettwaxdam i keč.

**🖼️ ASEGZEM N TEWLAFT DEG IḌUDAN-NNEK:**  
S usegzem-nneɣ afessus maca izeddig, ssewjed tiwlafin-nnek. Gzem, beddel tiddi, susem, qleb, ɣer, sseqdec ifiltren ifazen, akk war ma txellṣed tudert-nnek tusligt. Ḥkem timkta-nnek am zund qbel.

**🌐 TUDERT TUSLIGT D AMEZWARU, DIMA:**  
Tudert-nnek tusligt tga d aqbur. Ffar i yimezrawen yettaken isefka. Simon-Galleria ad ak-yefk anbaḍ. Kkes metadata EXIF am tikwatin GPS d isallen n tkamirat, ṭṭef timkta-nnek i keč.

**🔒 TAWELLIT TẒAYRIṬ:**  
Sɣel timkta-nnek s PIN, neɣ asekkil, neɣ adhm n uḍad. Ḥrez tiwlafin, ividyuten neɣ asnas akk – keč ad tfernded w'id yezmren ad yekcem. Lhna n wul, d amgal.

**🔄 ALSI S TWELLIT:**  
Enefê, iɣucen ttmagaren! Tamdint n usuddes n Simon-Galleria tettaǧǧa-k ad talseḍ tiwlafin d ividyuten i yettwakkesen deg kra n tesdatin. War ǧǧin txuṣṣ tiɣmiwin, ɣas lferḥ.

**🎨 TALLERYA-NNEK, ADDA-NNEK:**  
Seggem udem, ahulfu d tallalt akken ad iwulmen adda-nnek. Seg UI themes ɣer tiqemmuwin n wammud, Simon-Galleria ad ak-yefk tlelli n uxeddim i tesniremed.

**📷 TLELLI N IFMATEN IZDUMAZ:**  
JPEG, JPEG XL, PNG, MP4, MKV, RAW, SVG, GIF, AVIF, ividyuten d wiyaḍ – neṭṭef timkta-nnek deg wafmat i fernded. War tilisa, ɣas tirmitin ur nlimiten.

**✨ MATERIAL DESIGN S THEMES TUDDIN:**  
Wali ifaz n material design i yeqqim s themes tuddin. Tebɣid ugar? Kcem ɣer themes usligen acku ad tg tallerya-nnek d tazedgant s tidet.
